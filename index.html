Acupuncture 针灸

Fine, sterile needles placed at key points to ease pain, calm the nervous system, and restore your body's natural balance.

$150 initial treatment 60 minutes · $100 follow-up session 30 minutes
Package: 5 sessions $400 · $80/visit 30 minutes

We accept major insurances like UnitedHealthcare and Aetna. Please send us your insurance policy info via text message.

Traditional View TCM practitioners believe vital energy (Qi) flows through the body along pathways called meridians. Illness or pain occurs when these pathways are blocked, and needles are placed to clear the blockages and restore balance.

Scientific View Western medicine suggests acupuncture stimulates the nervous system, potentially triggering the release of natural painkillers (endorphins) and neurotransmitters such as serotonin. It is recognized by organizations like the National Center for Complementary and Integrative Health (NCCIH) as effective for a variety of conditions, including:

  • Back, joint, and neck pain
  • Migraines and tension headaches
  • Osteoarthritis
